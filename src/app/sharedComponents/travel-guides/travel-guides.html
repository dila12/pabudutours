Plan a smoother private tour in Sri Lanka

These notes cover the questions travellers ask Pabudu Tours most often before a private round tour or day trip. Rules can change, so treat visa and park details as a starting point and we will confirm the latest requirements when you book.

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka has two monsoon patterns. The south and west coasts (Kalutara, Bentota, Galle, Mirissa) are generally driest from December to April. The Cultural Triangle and east are often more comfortable from May to September. Wildlife safaris run year-round; whale watching from Mirissa is typically November to April.

Getting around with a private driver

Distances look short on a map but mountain roads and site stops make a private chauffeur the most efficient way to travel. A typical day links two or three highlights without hotel changes until evening. Browse day tours and multi-day packages, or start with Sigiriya, Kandy, Ella or a Udawalawa safari.

Visas, money and packing

  • Most visitors apply online for an ETA before arrival. Check the official immigration site for your passport.
  • Sri Lankan rupees are used everywhere. Cards work in hotels and larger towns; keep cash for small shops and tips.
  • Pack light layers, a hat, modest clothing for temples (shoulders and knees covered), and comfortable walking shoes for Sigiriya and Ella.
  • Temple visits require removing shoes and hats. Safari jeeps are open-sided — bring sun protection and a light jacket at dawn.

How many days do you need?

Two or three days covers Sigiriya, Kandy and a first safari. Five to seven days adds Ella, tea country and the south coast. Ten days is enough for a full island loop including Polonnaruwa, Mirissa and Galle. Compare the 5 day, 7 day and 10 day private tours, then message us if you want hotels or pace adjusted.
